One shaded boat that seats 14, three beach days from 9am to 5pm. Pickup and drop-off from Belize or San Pedro is available, day or night, for a small fee.

How it works

  1. 1

    Pick your beach

    Choose Secret Beach, Goff's Caye or Miami Beach for a day on the water, 9am to 5pm. Every trip starts in Caye Caulker.

  2. 2

    Board the shaded boat

    The boat has a big canopy for shade, seats 14 and has dry compartments. Pickup from Belize or San Pedro is a small fee.

  3. 3

    Eat, sip and relax

    Rum punch and seasonal ceviche come with every trip: conch, lobster or shrimp, your pick when it is in season.

BEACHES

Three beaches, one shaded boat. Goff's Caye and Miami Beach add lobster, chicken, fish or vegetarian food.
